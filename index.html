About

Specialists in real assets

Kingdom Estates is a privately owned LLC headquartered in Atlanta, GA, with affiliate offices in Dallas, TX and Tel Aviv, Israel.

We focus on the acquisition, development, management and value-add of real estate — executing investment strategies while enhancing assets, creating the future we want and the one you deserve.

Founded in 2020 after multiple investors sought to trust us with their funds, we hold an average of $100 million in assets and manage over $30 million of private and institutional equity — and have always exceeded expectations.
